# UNFRAME

A short interactive experience by LENSVRA where viewers discover how digital framing shapes their first impression of a person.

## The Unique Kernel
The viewer commits to an answer before they know the truth. They watch the same person in the same moment three times, each through a different emotional lens with matching music, and say what mood they see. Only at the end do they learn which treatment each clip had and see the original, unfiltered clip, next to their own answers.

A video could show the three lenses. Only an interactive piece can ask, remember what you said, and hand it back to you.

## Who It's For
LENSVRA's own audience: people who already use and enjoy Snapchat lenses and rarely stop to ask what a lens does to how they read a face. Today they see filtered content and react to it without noticing the filter.

## The Core Loop
1. Start the experience from a short introduction inviting them to watch and share their impression.
2. Watch a short clip, with no label saying which treatment it has.
3. Answer: "How would you describe this person's mood?" Happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, or unsure.
4. Repeat for the second and third clip.
5. Reach the reveal: the original, unfiltered clip, which treatment each clip had, the viewer's own answers, and the option to replay and compare.
6. End on the reflection question.

It is a one-time journey of about a minute, not something to return to daily. People pass it on because of the reveal.

## Inspiration & Identity
- The creator's own practice: Snapchat lenses (LENSVRA), original music and lyrics, choreography and dance.
- The song is about being **ONLY HUMAN**; two versions exist and the creator mixes the music.
- Footage: the creator dancing by the ocean.
- Tone: reflective, not a quiz with right and wrong. "Each person has their own opinion and taste of both music and art, and also different feelings."

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"An opportunity to turn my creative skills into a working app with the help of an AI agent," while understanding the steps along the way. It brings together the lenses being made right now, a new song, creativity and technology, to share with the whole audience so they "question how the digital effects and music actually influence their first impression and feelings."

## What "Working" Looks Like
A viewer can complete this journey:

1. Start the experience.
2. Watch three short clips from one setting, each with a different emotional lens and matching music.
3. Record their impression after each clip, without seeing the treatment labels beforehand.
4. Reach a reveal screen showing the original, unfiltered clip, which treatment they saw and their answers, with the option to replay and compare.
5. Reflect on the question: "Are you reacting to what happened, or to how it was presented?"

The answers must carry through correctly to the reveal screen, and the video, audio, and navigation must work throughout.

Success means the experience works and invites reflection, even if someone's answers do not change.

**The compelling moment:** the reveal, when the viewer sees their own three answers next to the three treatments and the unfiltered original of one moment.

## The POC Boundary
- One setting: dancing by the ocean.
- Four short clips: happy, sad, angry, and the unfiltered original (shown only at the reveal). The lens and the music are prepared by the creator and are part of each video file; the app plays them.
- One question after each clip, with six answers: happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, unsure.
- One reveal screen with the unfiltered original, the treatments, the viewer's answers, and replay.
- One closing reflection question.
- If the three clips are separate takes rather than one identical recording, the reveal says clearly that they are similar takes, not identical footage.
- Runs on the creator's computer for the demo video; code in a public GitHub repository.

## Explicitly Cut
- **Accounts and saved results.** Answers only need to live until the reveal; storing them adds work and privacy questions without proving the idea.
- **Live camera or applying lenses inside the app.** The lenses live in Snapchat; the app plays finished clips. Building lens technology is far outside a few hours.
- **Scoring answers as right or wrong.** The point is reflection, not a test.

## Open Question
- **One recording or three takes.** To be tested by the creator: can the same ocean recording be put through all three lenses? The fallback (similar takes, stated honestly) is already agreed.
