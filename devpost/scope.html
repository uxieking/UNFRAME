UNFRAME

A short interactive experience where viewers discover how digital framing shapes their first impression of a person.

The unique kernel

The viewer commits to an answer before they know the truth. Same person, same moment, three emotional lenses with matching music. Only at the end do they see which treatment each clip had and the original, unfiltered clip, next to their own answers.

A video could show the three lenses. Only an interactive piece can ask, remember what you said, and hand it back to you.

Who and why

Who it's for

LENSVRA's own audience: people who already use and enjoy Snapchat lenses and rarely stop to ask what a lens does to how they read a face.

Why it matters to you

Bringing together your lenses, your new song, creativity and technology.

"An opportunity to turn my creative skills into a working app with the help of an AI agent."

Identity

Your lenses, your choreography, your song about being ONLY HUMAN. Reflective in tone: no right or wrong answers.

What "working" looks like

  1. Start the experience.
  2. Watch three short clips from one setting, each with a different emotional lens and matching music.
  3. Record an impression after each clip, without seeing the treatment labels beforehand.
  4. Reach a reveal screen showing the original, unfiltered clip, the treatments and the answers, with the option to replay and compare.
  5. Reflect on: "Are you reacting to what happened, or to how it was presented?"

Answers must carry through correctly to the reveal, and video, audio and navigation must work throughout. Success means the experience works and invites reflection, even if someone's answers do not change.

The boundary

Now: in the prototype7
  • One setting: dancing by the ocean.
  • Four short clips: happy, sad, angry, and the unfiltered original (shown only at the reveal). Lens and music are prepared by you and are part of each video file; the app plays them.
  • One question after each clip, six answers: happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, unsure.
  • One reveal screen with the unfiltered original, treatments, answers and replay.
  • One closing reflection question.
  • If the clips are separate takes, the reveal says clearly they are similar takes, not identical footage.
  • Runs on your computer for the demo video; code in a public GitHub repository.

  • Accounts and saved results. Answers only need to live until the reveal.
