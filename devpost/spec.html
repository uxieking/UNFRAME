UNFRAME — how it's built

One web page, three small files, and a folder with your four clips. Nothing to install, nothing to log in to.

The pieces

index.html

What is on the page.

The name, the texts, the buttons, and the place where a video plays. All five screens live here; only one shows at a time.

style.css

How it looks.

The dark cinema background, the purple, white and grey, the sharp modern text.

script.js

What happens.

Plays the next clip, remembers each answer in a short list, and builds the reveal.

media/

Your four clips.

Angry, sad, happy, normal. The lens and music are already inside each file. The page only plays them.

How they work together

The journey through the pieces

What the page remembers

One short list, one line per lens clip. For example:

ClipChosenReveal shows
angrysad"You said: sad"
sadunsure"You said: unsure"
happyskipped"You skipped this one"

It lives only while the page is open. Reloading or pressing "start again" empties it. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Running it and showing it

To start it

Open the project folder and double-click index.html. No server, no keys, no accounts.

For the demo video

Open it in Chrome, make the window tall and narrow like a phone, and record the screen with sound. Upload to YouTube with comments off.

Online later, optional

GitHub Pages can publish this same folder as a link. Not part of this build.

Your question: making the opening eye-catching

"I'm not sure where to place it at the site to get the design correct for eye-catching immediately and make the users want to test it."

UNFRAME is its own page, so the opening screen is the whole first impression. The approach: one name, one short text, one button, all visible without scrolling. The start button is the only purple thing on the screen, so the eye goes to it.

How we check it during the build: you look at it on your own phone, then hand it to one other person without explaining anything. If they press start within a few seconds, it works. If not, we adjust.

Where it could break

A clip won't play

The clip area says "This clip could not be played." and the question still appears.

No sound

A small "sound on" reminder before the first clip. Sound is tested on your own phone during the build.

Slow clips

Keep each clip short and under about 10 MB. GitHub refuses single files over 100 MB.

Decisions

Yours
  • A simple web page, not an installed app.
  • Vertical clips; most of your audience opens it on a phone.
  • The four clips, including the song, may be public on GitHub. "I'm a public person."
  • The demo video goes on YouTube with comments off.
  • Filming on 2 or 3 October 2026.
  • One question per clip, about the person on screen. The question about the viewer's own feelings was removed as "the most personal one."
  • No privacy note on the page. Nothing is saved or sent, and a note could make viewers doubt it.
  • The normal clip has no music, no text, and no name on the reveal. "Because the user have to think."
  • Your four real clips are in the media folder as .mp4. Stand-in clips are no longer needed.
My recommendations, which you accepted
  • No framework and no added libraries. Every line in the project belongs to UNFRAME, and nothing needs installing.
  • Stand-in clips if we build before the real ones exist. Plainly labelled, replaced before the demo video.
  • All the words in one block at the top of script.js. You can change wording yourself without touching anything else.
  • One sentence in the README saying UNFRAME does not save or send any answers. For the curious, without disturbing the experience.
Simplified on purpose
  • Opened from your computer instead of a website with a link.
  • Answers kept in memory instead of being saved.
  • Finished video files instead of lenses running in the page.
Still open, none blocking the build
  • One recording or three takes. A yes/no setting adds the "similar takes" line to the reveal.
  • Final wording of the opening text.
