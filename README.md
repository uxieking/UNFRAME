# UNFRAME

*A short interactive experience by LENSVRA.*

You watch the same moment three times, each through a different emotional lens with matching music, and say what mood you see. Only at the end do you find out which lens each clip had, see the unfiltered original, and get your own answers handed back.

> Are you reacting to what happened, or to how it was presented?

## How to open it

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the folder and double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser, such as Chrome.
3. Turn the sound on. The music is part of the framing.

There is nothing to install and nothing to log in to. The page works best held upright on a phone or in a tall, narrow browser window.

## Privacy

UNFRAME does not save or send any answers. They exist only while the page is open and disappear when you reload or start again.

## What's inside

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | What is on the page: the opening screen, the clip screen and the reveal |
| `style.css` | How it looks: cinema dark, purple, white and grey |
| `script.js` | What happens: all the texts and settings at the top, then the journey |
| `media/` | The four clips: angry, sad, happy (each with its lens and music) and normal |

To change any wording, edit the texts block at the top of `script.js`.
